About Close Enough

The game

One new case every day at midnight Eastern Time: five questions about the real world, 20 seconds each. Answer with a range. If the true number lands inside it, you score, and the tighter the range, the more you score. Miss and you get 0.

Where the numbers come from

Every answer comes from a published source, shown under each question when it's revealed. Every case is checked by a person before it goes live. Main sources:

  • Our World in Data (CC BY 4.0), including data from Gapminder and the UN
  • United Nations: World Population Prospects, World Urbanization Prospects, UNESCO, FAO, UNEP
  • World Bank: World Development Indicators, Global Findex, Poverty and Inequality Platform
  • WHO / UNICEF, ITU, GSMA Intelligence, IEA / Tracking SDG7, Ember, USGS, NIH, peer-reviewed studies

Spotted a number that looks wrong? Email andrewgmaxwell123@gmail.com and it gets checked.

Privacy

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies.
  • Your streak and today's result are saved only in your own browser. Clearing your browser data erases them.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard short-term server logs to run and protect the site.
  • We don't collect your name, email, or location, and we don't sell anything about you.
